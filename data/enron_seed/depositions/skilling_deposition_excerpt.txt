DEPOSITION OF JEFFREY K. SKILLING
Taken April 9, 2002
Case No. CV-2002-0043

Q. Mr. Skilling, were you present at the Raptor II finance meeting in March of 2001?
A. I was.

Q. What was the date of that meeting?
A. I believe it was March 12, 2001. My calendar confirms that.

Q. Who attended?
A. Robert Smith, Andrew Fastow, and myself.

Q. Did you personally authorize a wire transfer to Raptor II following the meeting?
A. Yes, I approved a $2.5 million transfer. Bob Smith executed it on my behalf.
