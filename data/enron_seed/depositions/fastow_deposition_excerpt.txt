DEPOSITION OF ANDREW S. FASTOW
Taken April 2, 2002
Case No. CV-2002-0043

Q. Mr. Fastow, were you present at the Raptor II finance committee meeting?
A. I was present, yes.

Q. What was the date of that meeting?
A. March 15, 2001.

Q. Who else attended?
A. Jeff Skilling was there. Bob Smith was not present — he was out of the office that week.

Q. Did you personally authorize the wire transfer to Raptor II?
A. I did not. The transfer was approved by Mr. Skilling directly, not by me.

Q. What was the amount of the wire transfer?
A. I recall it being approximately $5 million, not the smaller figure some documents suggest.
