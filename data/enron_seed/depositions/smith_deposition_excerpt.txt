DEPOSITION OF ROBERT K. SMITH
Taken March 14, 2002
Case No. CV-2002-0043

Q. Mr. Smith, did you attend the finance committee meeting regarding the Raptor II vehicle?
A. Yes, I did.

Q. When was that meeting?
A. To the best of my recollection, it was March 12, 2001.

Q. And who else was present?
A. Jeffrey Skilling attended, along with Andrew Fastow and myself.

Q. Was there a wire transfer discussed?
A. Yes. A $2.5 million transfer to Raptor II. I signed off on it personally at Jeff's request.

Q. Did you have written authorization from Mr. Skilling?
A. The approval came via email on March 9, 2001.
