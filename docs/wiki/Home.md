## Product Context

Product: AI-powered legal discovery prototype for cross-document review of case materials.

Core demo case: Enron / Raptor II legal discovery corpus with depositions, emails, and an internal memo.

Primary user in testing: a legal reviewer, litigation support user, investigator, or other analyst who must compare statements across many documents and find contradictions quickly.
